MyPascoConnect Not Working — Troubleshooting Guide

Work through this guide before calling the help desk. Most MyPascoConnect issues have simple fixes that take less than 2 minutes.

🔓 Quick Fix Checklist

Try these first (takes 60 seconds):
  • ✓ Using Google Chrome? (not Safari, Firefox, or Edge)
  • ✓ Is your internet connection working? (try another site)
  • ✓ Is CAPS LOCK off? Passwords are case-sensitive
  • ✓ Username is your Student ID number, not email address
  • ✓ Try an Incognito window (Ctrl+Shift+N in Chrome)

Common Issues & Fixes

🔒 Can’t Log In — “Incorrect username or password”

Students:

  • Username = your Student ID / lunch number (digits only, no email)
  • Password = your LDAP password (ask teacher to reset if forgotten)
  • Check CAPS LOCK and keyboard language settings

Staff:

  • Username = district email (e.g. firstname.lastname@pasco.k12.fl.us)
  • Password = district network/Active Directory password
  • Call IT at 813-794-2859 if locked out
📋 Page Won’t Load / Blank White Screen

A blank screen is almost always a browser cache or extension problem. Fix it in order:

  1. Open a Chrome Incognito window (Ctrl+Shift+N) and try logging in
  2. If incognito works: clear your browser cache â€” Chrome menu › Settings › Privacy › Clear browsing data › Cached images/files + Cookies › All time
  3. Disable Chrome extensions (especially ad blockers) — they can block ClassLink scripts
  4. Try on a different device to confirm if it is device-specific
  5. Restart your router if no sites load
⚡ Page Loads Very Slowly
  • Check your internet speed — ClassLink requires a stable connection (minimum 5 Mbps recommended)
  • Close unnecessary browser tabs to free up memory
  • On school Chromebooks: restart the device and try again
  • Contact your school IT if school network seems slow during peak hours
🚫 “Your account has been locked”

Accounts lock after too many failed login attempts as a security measure.

  • Students: Wait 15–30 minutes, then try again with the correct password. If still locked, ask your teacher to unlock the account.
  • Staff: Call IT at 813-794-2859 immediately — locked staff accounts can be unlocked remotely.
📊 My LaunchPad is Empty — No Apps Show
  • This often happens at the start of a new school year before apps are re-assigned
  • Your teacher needs to confirm you are enrolled in the correct classes in the district system
  • A fresh school year may require IT to provision your apps — allow 1–2 school days
  • Confirm you logged in with the student account, not a parent or guest session
🎈 Specific App Won’t Open From LaunchPad
  • Log out of MyPascoConnect completely, wait 1 minute, and log back in — SSO tokens expire
  • Try clicking the app again — first click sometimes fails due to token generation delay
  • If a specific app (e.g. Canvas) has an error, the issue may be on that app’s server — try again in 15 minutes
  • Report persistent app errors to your teacher so they can escalate to IT
📘 Canvas MyLearning Won’t Load
  • Try opening Canvas directly at pasco.instructure.com instead of through the LaunchPad
  • Clear cookies for instructure.com in Chrome settings
  • Disable the Canvas extension if installed and use the browser version instead
  • Check if the issue is a specific course or all of Canvas — a single course issue is teacher-side
💻 Microsoft Office 365 / Teams Problems
  • Sign out of any personal Microsoft accounts in Chrome before clicking the Office 365 tile
  • If prompted to sign in again, use your school email (e.g. studentid@students.pasco.k12.fl.us)
  • For Teams video not working: check Chrome has permission for camera and microphone (site settings)
  • If Teams app is broken, use the browser version at teams.microsoft.com
📺 Chromebook-Specific Issues
  • Sign out of your personal Google account and sign in to your school Google account
  • On a school-managed Chromebook: do not modify managed settings or disable extensions
  • Clear Chrome data: Settings › Privacy › Clear browsing data
  • If credentials are cached wrong: Settings › People › Sync and Google services › clear data
  • As a last resort: Powerwash the Chromebook (Settings › Advanced › Reset settings) — only do this with IT approval
📱 Problems on Phone or Tablet
  • Use the ClassLink app (available on iOS App Store and Google Play) rather than a browser on mobile
  • In a mobile browser, Chrome for Android and Safari for iOS are supported
  • Some LaunchPad apps may be limited on small screens — use a larger device for full functionality
  • Clear app cache in your phone settings if ClassLink app is behaving oddly
🌏 Works at School But Not at Home
  • Check your home internet connection — try a speed test
  • Some home routers have content filtering that may block ClassLink — try on mobile data
  • DNS issues: try changing your DNS to 8.8.8.8 (Google DNS) in your router settings
  • VPN software may interfere — temporarily disable your VPN and retry
📅 New School Year — Can’t Log In After Summer
  • Student credentials (especially LDAP passwords) may be reset over summer — ask your teacher
  • Your Student ID remains the same — only the password may change
  • LaunchPad apps will be re-assigned at the start of term — check with teacher if LaunchPad is empty
  • New school? Your account may have transferred — verify with the new school’s office
