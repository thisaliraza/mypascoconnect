What is MyPascoConnect?

MyPascoConnect is the official Single Sign-On (SSO) portal for Pasco County Schools in Florida. It gives students, teachers, and staff secure access to all their digital learning tools from one personalized dashboard called the LaunchPad.

🔑

One Login

Log in once and access every school application without remembering multiple passwords.

📚

Learning Tools

Direct access to Canvas (MyLearning), digital textbooks, library resources, and more.

💼

Microsoft 365

Full Office 365 suite available in-browser and for download at home.

📱

Any Device

Works on Chromebooks, tablets, phones, and desktops with any modern browser.

Step-by-Step Login Guide

Follow these steps to log in to your MyPascoConnect account.

1

Open a Web Browser

Launch Google Chrome (recommended) on your device. Make sure your browser is up to date for the best experience.

2

Navigate to the Portal

In the address bar, go to: mypascoconnect.pasco.k12.fl.us

3

Click "Sign in to myPascoConnect"

On the portal landing page, click the large "Sign in to myPascoConnect" button to proceed to the login screen.

4

Enter Your Credentials

UsernameYour Student ID Number (also your lunch number)
PasswordYour assigned LDAP password

First-time users receive their LDAP password from their teacher or school.

5

Access Your LaunchPad

After a successful login you will be taken to your personalized LaunchPad — your dashboard of all available apps. Click any icon to launch that tool instantly.

QR

QR Badge Login (At School)

If your school provided a QR badge, you can scan it at school devices to log in instantly — no typing required.

Troubleshooting and Help

Having trouble logging in? Here are the most common issues and how to fix them.

Forgot my password

Students: Ask your classroom teacher to reset your LDAP password.

Parents: Log into the myStudent parent portal to retrieve or reset your child's credentials.

Staff: Call the tech help desk at 813-794-2859.

Don't know my username

Your username is your Student ID Number (also your lunch number). Find it on your student ID card, report card, or by asking your teacher.

The page won't load
  • Refresh the page (F5 or Ctrl+R)
  • Clear your browser cache and cookies
  • Switch to Google Chrome
  • Disable browser extensions
  • Try incognito/private window
Can't log in on mobile

Try Chrome on Android or Safari on iOS. Make sure your browser and OS are up to date.

Account locked or access denied

Wait 15–30 minutes and try again. Contact your teacher or call 813-794-2859.

An app in my LaunchPad is not working
  • Log out and back in to refresh your session
  • Try a different browser
  • Clear cookies for that app's domain
  • Contact your teacher or school IT

Frequently Asked Questions

Is MyPascoConnect free to use?

Yes! MyPascoConnect is provided at no cost to all Pasco County Schools students, teachers, and staff.

Can I use MyPascoConnect at home?

Absolutely. The portal is accessible 24/7 from any device with an internet connection.

What is an LDAP password?

LDAP is the system Pasco County Schools uses to manage user accounts. Your LDAP password is assigned by the school. If you don't know yours, ask your teacher.

What is the LaunchPad?

The LaunchPad is your personalized home dashboard inside MyPascoConnect. After logging in you'll see app icons for all your educational tools. Click any icon to go to that app without a separate login.

Can parents log in using the same portal?

Parents use a separate portal called myStudent to view grades, attendance, and retrieve student credentials.

Does MyPascoConnect work on Chromebooks?

Yes. Chromebooks with Google Chrome work great with MyPascoConnect and most LaunchPad apps.

How do I change my password?

Students cannot change their LDAP password on their own. Ask your teacher or call the help desk at 813-794-2859.
