What It Is

What is MyPascoConnect?

MyPascoConnect is the official Single Sign-On (SSO) portal for Pasco County Schools in Florida, powered by ClassLink. One secure login gives students, teachers, and staff access to every digital learning tool they need.

🔓

One Login

Sign in once to your ClassLink LaunchPad and access every school app without separate passwords.

📚

Canvas MyLearning

Access assignments, grades, course materials, and class discussions through Pasco’s LMS.

💻

Microsoft 365

Full Office suite online — Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Teams, OneDrive — free for all students.

📱

Any Device

Works on Chromebooks, iPhones, Android, tablets, and desktops with Google Chrome.

Step by Step

How to Log In to MyPascoConnect

Follow these steps to access your ClassLink LaunchPad on any device.

1

Open Google Chrome

Launch Google Chrome on your Chromebook, laptop, phone, or tablet. Chrome is the recommended browser for the best compatibility with ClassLink and all LaunchPad apps.

2

Go to the Official Portal

In the address bar, navigate to the Pasco County Schools ClassLink portal:

🌐 login.classlink.com/my/dsbpc
3

Click “Sign In to MyPascoConnect”

On the portal landing page, click the large Sign in to MyPascoConnect button to reach the credential entry screen.

4

Enter Your Username and Password

👤 UsernameYour Student ID Number (also called your lunch number — usually 6–7 digits)
🔒 PasswordYour school-assigned LDAP password (ask your teacher if unknown)
💡 First time? Your school will provide a temporary LDAP password. You may be prompted to change it on first login.
5

Access Your ClassLink LaunchPad

After a successful login, your personalized ClassLink LaunchPad loads. Click any app tile — Canvas, Office 365, Teams — to open it instantly with no extra login required.

QR

QR Badge Login (At School)

Some Pasco County Schools issue QR badge cards. Scan yours at a school device to log in instantly without typing.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is MyPascoConnect the same as ClassLink?

Yes. MyPascoConnect is Pasco County Schools’ branded name for their ClassLink LaunchPad. ClassLink is the underlying Single Sign-On platform. When you log in at the Pasco portal, you are signing in to ClassLink.

What is my MyPascoConnect username?

Students use their Student ID number — also called their lunch number (usually 6–7 digits). You can find it on your student ID card, report card, or by asking your teacher. Staff use their district email or assigned username.

What is an LDAP password?

LDAP (Lightweight Directory Access Protocol) is the system Pasco County Schools uses to manage all user accounts on the district network. Your LDAP password is assigned or set by your school. It is not a password you create yourself — ask your teacher if you do not know yours.

Can I use MyPascoConnect from home?

Yes. The MyPascoConnect ClassLink portal is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week from any device with an internet connection. Students can access Canvas, Office 365, and their digital textbooks from home, a library, or anywhere with Wi-Fi.

How do I reset my MyPascoConnect password?

Students: ask your classroom teacher to reset your LDAP password. Parents: use the myStudent parent portal or contact your child’s school. Staff: call the Pasco tech help desk at 813-794-2859. See our full password reset guide for detailed steps.

Does MyPascoConnect work on Chromebook?

Yes. Chromebooks are the most common student devices in Pasco County Schools. MyPascoConnect and all ClassLink LaunchPad apps work fully in Google Chrome on Chrome OS. See our Chromebook login guide.

Can parents log in to MyPascoConnect?

Parents use a separate portal called myStudent, powered by Focus School Software. It is different from the student MyPascoConnect ClassLink portal. Through myStudent, parents can view grades, attendance, and retrieve student credentials. See our parent login guide.
