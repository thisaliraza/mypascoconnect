MyPascoConnect on Chromebook — Chrome OS Login Guide

Chromebooks are the most common student device in Pasco County Schools. Here’s everything you need to know about using MyPascoConnect on Chrome OS.

Unofficial guide. Not affiliated with Pasco County Schools, ClassLink, or Google.

Why Chromebooks Work Best for MyPascoConnect

Pasco County Schools has distributed school-issued Chromebooks to students across the district. These devices run Chrome OS with Google Chrome built in — which is exactly the browser that ClassLink recommends for MyPascoConnect. Together, they create a seamless experience.

📺

Chrome Built-In

No browser installation needed — Chrome is the default and best browser for ClassLink.

⚡

Fast Login

School-managed Chromebooks may have ClassLink extension pre-installed for instant access.

🔒

School Managed

District IT configures settings and apps automatically on school-issued Chromebooks.

🍁

Lightweight

Chrome OS is faster and simpler than Windows or macOS for basic school tasks.

Step-by-Step Login on Chromebook

1

Sign In with Your School Google Account

At the Chromebook login screen, sign in with your school-issued Google account (e.g. studentid@students.pasco.k12.fl.us) if not already signed in. On a school-managed device, your account may already be listed.

2

Open Google Chrome

Chrome opens automatically. It is the default browser on all Chromebooks and the best choice for MyPascoConnect.

3

Go to the Portal

🌐 login.classlink.com/my/dsbpc
📌 Bookmark this URL in Chrome on your Chromebook for quick daily access.
4

Click “Sign In to MyPascoConnect”

Click the main sign-in button on the ClassLink landing page.

5

Enter Student ID & LDAP Password

Type your Student ID number (lunch number) as username and your LDAP password. Check CAPS LOCK is off before entering your password.

6

LaunchPad Loads

Your ClassLink LaunchPad appears. Click Canvas, Office 365, Teams, or any app tile to open it instantly with no additional login.

Using Canvas on Chromebook

Canvas MyLearning works fully in the Chrome browser on Chromebook — no app installation needed. Click the Canvas tile on your LaunchPad to open your courses immediately.

  • Submit assignments, view grades, and message teachers all in the browser
  • Watch lesson videos directly in Canvas without additional plugins
  • Optionally install the Canvas Student Chrome extension for notifications
  • File uploads from your Chromebook Downloads folder or Google Drive work seamlessly

Using Office 365 on Chromebook

Microsoft Office 365 works excellently on Chromebooks — all apps run in the Chrome browser with no installation required.

  • Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Teams, and Outlook all work in the browser
  • Files save automatically to OneDrive cloud storage
  • Microsoft Teams video calls and meetings work in Chrome on Chromebook
  • You can install the Microsoft Teams Progressive Web App from Teams.microsoft.com for a more native experience

Chromebook Troubleshooting

Can’t log in on school Chromebook
  • Make sure you signed in at the Chromebook login screen with your school Google account, not your personal one
  • Try clearing Chrome browser cache: Settings › Privacy › Clear browsing data
  • Ask your teacher — they may need to reset your LDAP password
Apps not loading on LaunchPad
  • Log out of MyPascoConnect completely and log back in
  • Try opening the app in a new tab directly
  • Check if other apps on the LaunchPad work — if all are broken it may be a server issue
Cached wrong credentials on Chromebook
  • Go to Chrome Settings › Passwords and remove any saved MyPascoConnect passwords
  • Sign out of Chrome and back in with your school Google account
  • If on a shared Chromebook, always use Guest mode or log in with your own account
Should I Powerwash my school Chromebook?

Do not Powerwash a school-managed Chromebook without IT approval. Powerwash (factory reset) on a managed device will remove all district configurations and may prevent you from logging back in. Contact IT at 813-794-2859 if you think a reset is needed.
