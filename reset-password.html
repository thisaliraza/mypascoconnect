MyPascoConnect Forgot Password — Reset Guide

Password recovery is handled differently for students, parents, and staff. Find your role below for the exact steps to regain access to your account.

🚫 Security Warning: Never enter your password on any unofficial website. Only reset your MyPascoConnect credentials through official school channels described below. This site cannot reset passwords for you.

🎒 Student Password Reset

Students cannot self-reset their LDAP password. The district controls all student account passwords for security reasons.

1

Ask Your Classroom Teacher

Tell your teacher you need your LDAP password reset. This is the fastest and correct way. Teachers have access to a district admin tool that can reset student passwords within minutes.

2

Teacher Resets Your Password

Your teacher logs into the district admin system and resets your LDAP password. They will give you a new temporary password verbally or on a slip of paper.

3

Log In With the Temporary Password

Use your Student ID and the temporary password to log in to MyPascoConnect.

4

Change Your Password

You may be prompted to create a new personal LDAP password. Choose something you can remember but that classmates cannot guess. Do not use your name or birthday.

💡 Can’t reach your teacher? Try the school office or the school library staff. Any Pasco County Schools employee with admin access can reset a student password.

👪 Parent Password Reset (myStudent Portal)

Parent passwords are for the myStudent portal (Focus School Software), not the student ClassLink system. You can usually reset it yourself:

1

Go to the myStudent Login Page

🌐 pasco.focusschoolsoftware.com
2

Click “Forgot Password”

Click the Forgot Password link on the login page and enter the email address associated with your parent account.

3

Check Your Email

A password reset email will be sent to your registered address. Check your spam/junk folder if it doesn’t arrive within 5 minutes.

4

Click the Reset Link

Click the link in the email and create a new password for your myStudent account.

⚠️ If your email address has changed since you registered, contact your child’s school office to update your email in the district records before attempting a self-service reset.

Common Password Errors

🔒 “Incorrect username or password” error

Verify your username is your Student ID number (not your email). Check that CAPS LOCK is off. Passwords are case-sensitive. If you recently had your password reset, use the new temporary password.

🚫 “Account is locked” message

After too many failed login attempts, accounts are locked automatically for security. Wait 15–30 minutes, then try again. If still locked, ask your teacher (students) or call IT at 813-794-2859 (staff).

📱 Password works on school computer but not on MyPascoConnect

This can happen when a password reset is only partially synced across systems. Try logging in at a school computer first to force a sync, then retry the portal. If the issue persists, ask IT to do a full LDAP sync on your account.

I changed my password and now nothing works

When you change your LDAP password, it must update across all connected school systems. Log out of all devices, wait 5 minutes, then log back in with the new password on each device. Also update any saved passwords in your browser.

Password Best Practices

  • 🔒 Create a password you can remember — use a phrase or mix of words + numbers
  • 🚫 Never share your password with friends, classmates, or anyone else
  • 📺 Do not save passwords in Chrome on shared school computers
  • ❌ Do not use your name, birthday, or student ID as your password
  • 📌 Log out whenever you finish on a shared or public device
