MyPascoConnect Student Login Guide

Step-by-step instructions for Pasco County Schools students to sign in to the ClassLink LaunchPad portal using their Student ID and LDAP password.

Unofficial guide. This page is not affiliated with Pasco County Schools or ClassLink. Always sign in at the official portal and never share your password.

What is MyPascoConnect for Students?

MyPascoConnect is the Single Sign-On (SSO) portal for Pasco County Schools, built on the ClassLink platform. As a student, one login gives you access to your entire digital school environment — Canvas MyLearning, Microsoft Office 365, Teams, digital textbooks, and all district-approved apps — all from one personalized ClassLink LaunchPad.

What You Need to Log In

👤 UsernameYour Student ID Number (same as your lunch number)
🔒 PasswordYour school-assigned LDAP password
🌎 BrowserGoogle Chrome (strongly recommended)
📺Any device: Chromebook, laptop, phone, tablet, or desktop

Step-by-Step Login Instructions

1

Open Google Chrome

Launch Google Chrome on your device. If you are on a school-issued Chromebook, it may already be signed into your school Google account.

2

Navigate to the Portal

Type the official URL in Chrome’s address bar:

🌐 login.classlink.com/my/dsbpc
📌 Tip: Bookmark this URL so you can reach MyPascoConnect quickly every day.
3

Click “Sign In to MyPascoConnect”

On the ClassLink landing page, click the large Sign in to MyPascoConnect button. Do not click any other sign-in buttons on this page.

4

Enter Your Student ID Number

Type your Student ID number in the username field. This is the same number used for your school lunch account — usually 6 to 7 digits. Do not include your school name or any letters.

💡 Where to find your Student ID: On your student ID card, on your printed report card, or ask your homeroom teacher. It is not your email address.
5

Enter Your LDAP Password

Type your LDAP password carefully. Passwords are case-sensitive — check that CAPS LOCK is off. If you have never logged in before, your school should have given you a temporary password.

⚠️ If you don’t know your LDAP password, ask your classroom teacher — not your parents, and not IT directly. Teachers can reset student passwords through the admin panel.
6

Your LaunchPad Loads

After a successful login, your personalized ClassLink LaunchPad appears. Click any app tile to open it instantly — Canvas, Office 365, Teams, digital textbooks — all without entering another password.

About Your Student ID (Lunch Number)

Your Student ID number is a unique 6–7 digit number assigned to you by Pasco County Schools when you enrolled. It is the same number used for your school lunch account, which is why it is often called the lunch number. You will use this number as your MyPascoConnect username throughout your time in the district.

  • Found on your student ID card
  • Printed on your report card or progress report
  • Available from your homeroom teacher
  • Visible in the myStudent parent portal (for parents)

About Your LDAP Password

LDAP stands for Lightweight Directory Access Protocol — the system Pasco County Schools uses to manage all user accounts across the district network. Your LDAP password is set or assigned by your school, not chosen by you. It controls your access to all school computers, network drives, and MyPascoConnect.

Important: Your LDAP password is different from your personal Gmail, Apple ID, or home Wi-Fi password. Never share it with classmates. Your teacher can reset it if needed.

Canvas MyLearning

View assignments, submit work, check grades, join class discussions, and message teachers.

💻

Microsoft Office 365

Chromebook Users

Chromebooks with Google Chrome are the most common student devices in Pasco County Schools, and MyPascoConnect is fully optimized for Chrome OS. On school-managed Chromebooks, the ClassLink extension may already be installed, making sign-in even faster.

First-Time Login Tips

  • You may be asked to change your temporary password on your first login — follow the prompts
  • Bookmark the portal URL in Chrome for quick daily access
  • Do not save your password on shared school computers in a public area
  • Log out when you are done, especially on shared devices
  • If a new school year starts and you cannot log in, ask your teacher — credentials may be reset each year

Frequently Asked Questions

What is my MyPascoConnect username?

Your username is your Student ID number — also called your lunch number. It is a 6–7 digit number found on your student ID card or report card.

What is my LDAP password?

Your LDAP password is assigned by your school. If you are new or have forgotten it, ask your classroom teacher to reset it. Teachers can reset student passwords through the district admin system.

Can I log in from home?

Yes. MyPascoConnect is available 24/7 from any device with internet access. Open Chrome, go to the portal URL, and sign in with your Student ID and LDAP password.

Why is my LaunchPad empty after login?

If your LaunchPad shows no apps, it may be a new school year enrollment issue, or your account may not yet have apps assigned. Contact your teacher or the school office.

Is it safe to stay logged in on my personal Chromebook?

You can stay logged in on your personal device, but always log out on any shared device. ClassLink sessions do eventually expire for security.
