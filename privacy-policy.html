Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how the MyPascoConnect Guide (“this site”, “we”) handles information when you visit thisaliraza.github.io/mypascoconnect.

🔒 Short version: We do not collect, store, or process any personal information. This is a static informational website with no login forms, no account system, and no data collection.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • No names, email addresses, or contact information
  • No student ID numbers or school credentials (we have no login forms)
  • No payment information
  • No cookies set by this website
  • No account registration or user profiles

Third-Party Services

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (github.com). GitHub may collect standard web server logs (IP addresses, browser type, pages visited) per their own privacy policy at GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

If analytics are added in the future (such as Google Analytics), this policy will be updated to reflect that.

External Links

This site links to external websites including the official Pasco County Schools ClassLink portal, Focus School Software portal, and pasco.k12.fl.us. We have no control over those websites and their privacy practices. Review their respective privacy policies before entering any personal information.

Official Disclaimer

This website is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Pasco County Schools, the District School Board of Pasco County, ClassLink Inc., or any school district. It is an independent informational resource only.

Never enter your school login credentials on this or any unofficial website. Only log in at the official Pasco County Schools ClassLink portal: login.classlink.com/my/dsbpc.

Children’s Privacy

We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. This site provides informational content only and has no data collection mechanisms. Parents with concerns should contact their child’s school directly.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date at the top.

Contact

For questions about this site’s content (not for school account support), visit our About page for contact information. For official school support: staff should call 813-794-2859; students should ask their teacher; parents should contact the school office.
