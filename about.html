About This Guide

An independent, unofficial informational resource for the Pasco County Schools community.

Our Mission

The MyPascoConnect Guide was created to help Pasco County Schools students, parents, and staff quickly understand and navigate the MyPascoConnect ClassLink portal. Official documentation can sometimes be hard to find or difficult to understand — our goal is to present the information clearly and help anyone who needs it.

We cover step-by-step login instructions, password help, the apps on your LaunchPad, Chromebook usage, mobile access, the myStudent parent portal, troubleshooting guides, and an extensive FAQ.

Important Disclaimer

This site is unofficial and independent.
  • This website is NOT affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Pasco County Schools, the District School Board of Pasco County, ClassLink Inc., or any school district.
  • All information is provided for informational purposes only and may not reflect the most current portal features.
  • Always use the official Pasco County Schools ClassLink portal to sign in.
  • Never enter your login credentials on this website or any unofficial site.
  • For official support, students should contact their teacher; staff should call IT at 813-794-2859.

What We Cover

  • How to log in to MyPascoConnect (students, parents, staff)
  • Understanding Student ID numbers and LDAP passwords
  • ClassLink LaunchPad apps: Canvas, Office 365, Teams, Focus Gradebook
  • Chromebook and mobile device guides
  • Password reset steps for all roles
  • Troubleshooting common login errors
  • Focus School Software (myStudent) parent portal guide
  • 20+ frequently asked questions

How We Keep Information Current

We review and update guide content regularly based on Pasco County Schools announcements, ClassLink platform updates, and community feedback. Pages show a last-updated date in the site metadata. However, portal interfaces can change — always check with your school if something looks different than described.
