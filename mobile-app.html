MyPascoConnect on Mobile — ClassLink App Guide

There is no dedicated MyPascoConnect app — instead use the free ClassLink app to access your LaunchPad on iPhone, Android, and iPad.

Unofficial guide. Not affiliated with Pasco County Schools, ClassLink, Apple, or Google.

Which App Do I Need?

📱 MyPascoConnect does not have its own mobile app. The app to download is ClassLink — the platform that powers MyPascoConnect. It is free and available on both iOS and Android. After login, your LaunchPad and all your school apps are accessible.


iPhone & iPad

Download ClassLink from the Apple App Store. Works on iOS 14 and later.

Download on App Store ↗
🤖

Android Phone & Tablet

Download ClassLink from Google Play Store. Works on Android 8.0 and later.

Get it on Google Play ↗
🌐

Mobile Browser

You can also use the mobile browser. Open Chrome or Safari and go to login.classlink.com/my/dsbpc.

Setup on iPhone or iPad

1

Download ClassLink from App Store

Search for “ClassLink” in the Apple App Store, or tap the download link above. Install the free app (published by ClassLink Inc.).

2

Open ClassLink and Find Your School

Open the app and search for “Pasco County Schools” or “DSBPC”. Select it from the results to set your school district.

3

Log In with Your Credentials

👤 UsernameYour Student ID number (lunch number)
🔒 PasswordYour LDAP password
4

Access Your LaunchPad

Your personalized LaunchPad appears. Tap any app tile to open Canvas, Office 365, Teams, or other school apps.

5

Enable Biometric Login (Optional)

ClassLink supports Face ID and Touch ID on compatible iPhones and iPads. Enable this in the ClassLink app settings for faster future logins.

Setup on Android

1

Download ClassLink from Google Play

Search for “ClassLink” in the Google Play Store and install the free app.

2

Search for Pasco County Schools

Open ClassLink and search for your district: “Pasco County Schools” or “District School Board of Pasco County”.

3

Enter Your Student ID and LDAP Password

Sign in with your Student ID number as username and your LDAP password. Tap Sign In.

4

Enable Fingerprint Login (Optional)

On supported Android devices, you can enable fingerprint authentication in ClassLink settings for quicker logins.

What Works on Mobile

  • ✓ ClassLink LaunchPad — all your app tiles
  • ✓ Canvas MyLearning — view assignments, submit work, check grades
  • ✓ Microsoft Teams — class chats, meeting joins, file access
  • ✓ OneDrive — access your saved files
  • ✓ Office web apps — Word, Excel, PowerPoint in browser/app
  • ✓ Focus Gradebook — check grades and attendance
⚠️ Some features work better on a laptop or Chromebook. Typing long documents or submitting complex assignments is easier on a larger screen. Mobile is best for quick checks, notifications, and viewing content.

Tips for Mobile Users

  • 📌 Enable push notifications in ClassLink for assignment reminders
  • 📱 Download the Canvas Student app separately for better Canvas notifications
  • 📱 Download Microsoft Teams app for the best Teams video call experience
  • 🔒 Log out of ClassLink on any device you share with others
  • ⚡ Keep the ClassLink app updated for the latest features and security patches
