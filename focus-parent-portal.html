Focus School Software — Pasco County Schools Parent Portal Guide

The myStudent parent portal, powered by Focus School Software, is how Pasco County Schools parents monitor student progress and access school information.

Unofficial guide. Not affiliated with Pasco County Schools or Focus School Software.

What is Focus School Software?

Focus School Software is the Student Information System (SIS) used by Pasco County Schools and many other Florida school districts. It manages all student academic records, attendance, grade books, scheduling, and parent communication. For parents, Focus powers the myStudent parent portal — your window into your child’s school life.

How to Create a Parent Account

1

Go to the myStudent Portal

🌐 pasco.focusschoolsoftware.com
2

Click “Create a Parent Account”

Look for the Create an Account or New User option on the login page.

3

Enter Your Email Address

Use the email address you provided to the school during enrollment. This is how the portal verifies you are the parent on record.

4

Verify Your Email

Check your inbox for a verification email. Click the link inside. Check spam/junk if it doesn’t arrive.

5

Link Your Child’s Account

Enter your child’s Student ID number and date of birth. This links their school record to your parent account.

💡 Don’t know your child’s Student ID? Call the school office — they can provide it with proper ID verification.

6

Start Using the Portal

Log in with your email and password anytime to monitor your child’s academic progress.

What You Can See in myStudent

📊

Grades & GPA

Real-time grades for all courses, current GPA, historical report cards and progress reports.

📅

Attendance Record

Daily attendance by period, absences, tardies, excused/unexcused records, and patterns over time.

📋

Class Schedule

Full course schedule by semester including teacher names, room numbers, and class times.

📧

Teacher Communication

Send messages to teachers directly through the portal and receive school communications.

👤

Student ID & Info

Your child’s Student ID number (also their MyPascoConnect username), demographic info on file.

🆎

Lunch Account

Check remaining lunch balance and prepay online to ensure your child has account funds.

Managing Multiple Children

If you have more than one child in Pasco County Schools, you can link all of them to a single parent account. After linking the first child, navigate to account settings and add additional students by entering each child’s Student ID and date of birth.

Frequently Asked Questions

My email is not recognized when creating an account

Your email must match what the school has on file from enrollment. If you used a different email or it has changed, contact your child’s school office to update the record before trying again.

Can I reset my parent portal password myself?

Yes. Click Forgot Password on the myStudent login page and a reset link will be emailed to your registered address. If your email has changed, contact the school office.

I can’t see my child’s grades or information

This may happen if: (1) the Student ID or date of birth entered during linking was incorrect, (2) your account needs to be manually approved by the school, or (3) your child’s records have not been set up yet. Contact the school office for help.

Is there a mobile app for myStudent?

Focus School Software has a mobile-friendly web portal. The website works well on iOS and Android browsers. Search the App Store or Google Play for any official Focus School Software or myStudent Pasco app for a potential native experience.
