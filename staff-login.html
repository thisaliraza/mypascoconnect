MyPascoConnect Staff & Teacher Login Guide

How Pasco County Schools teachers and staff sign in to the MyPascoConnect ClassLink portal, manage classroom tools, and get account support.

Unofficial guide. Not affiliated with Pasco County Schools or ClassLink. For official IT support call 813-794-2859.

Staff & Teacher Portal Access

Teachers and staff at Pasco County Schools use the same MyPascoConnect ClassLink portal as students, but with district-issued staff credentials. Your login gives you a role-specific LaunchPad with teacher and administrative tools not available on student accounts.

What You Need to Log In

👤 UsernameYour district email address or staff account ID (e.g. jsmith@pasco.k12.fl.us)
🔒 PasswordYour district network / Active Directory password
📺Google Chrome recommended; works on Windows, Mac, Chromebook
📱MFA (Multi-Factor Authentication) may be required depending on district policy

Step-by-Step Staff Login

1

Open Google Chrome

Use Chrome on your school-issued device or personal computer. Staff can access MyPascoConnect from any internet-connected device.

2

Go to the Portal

Navigate to:

🌐 login.classlink.com/my/dsbpc
3

Click “Sign In to MyPascoConnect”

Click the sign-in button and choose the Staff/Teacher sign-in option if prompted.

4

Enter Your Credentials

Enter your district email (e.g. firstname.lastname@pasco.k12.fl.us) and district password. This is the same password used for school computers and Outlook email.

5

Complete MFA if Required

If Multi-Factor Authentication is enabled on your account, approve the login request via your authenticator app or district-provided method.

🔒 MFA is a security requirement for many staff accounts. Contact IT at 813-794-2859 if you need help setting it up.
6

Your Staff LaunchPad Loads

Your personalized staff LaunchPad appears with teacher-specific tools: Canvas teacher view, Outlook, Teams, Focus grade management, HR tools, and district resources.

Apps Available for Teachers & Staff

📘

Canvas (Teacher View)

Create courses, publish assignments, grade submissions, communicate with students and parents.

📧

Microsoft Outlook

District email for communicating with students, parents, admin, and colleagues.

💻

Microsoft Teams

Host virtual class sessions, department meetings, and collaborate with staff districtwide.

📊

Focus Grade Management

Enter grades, manage attendance, access student records, and generate reports.

💸

View pay stubs, update personal information, and manage HR-related tasks.

📂

Store lesson plans, documents, and resources in the Microsoft 365 cloud ecosystem.

New Staff Onboarding

If you are a new employee who has not yet received your district login credentials:

  1. Contact your school’s office manager or principal for your initial login details
  2. IT will set up your district email (e.g. firstname.lastname@pasco.k12.fl.us)
  3. You may need to set your initial password by following the link in a welcome email
  4. Call 813-794-2859 if you have not received credentials within your first week

Locked Out or Forgotten Password

⚠️ Do not ask students to help you reset your password. Your staff credentials have access to sensitive student data. Always contact IT through official channels.

For staff password resets:

  • Call the Pasco County Schools IT Help Desk: 813-794-2859
  • Be prepared to verify your identity with your employee ID or date of birth
  • IT will issue a temporary password which you must change on first use
  • After-hours support may have limited availability — plan ahead before major deadlines

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IT help desk number for Pasco County Schools staff?

The Pasco County Schools technology help desk for staff and teachers is 813-794-2859. Call this number for password resets, account lockouts, MFA setup, and general technical support.

Can I use my personal email to log in?

No. You must use your district-issued email address (e.g. firstname.lastname@pasco.k12.fl.us) as your username. Personal email addresses cannot be used to access district systems.

Can I access MyPascoConnect from home?

Yes. Staff can access MyPascoConnect and all connected district applications from any device with an internet connection. Some district resources may require VPN access — check with IT if you encounter access issues from off-campus.

How do I reset a student’s password?

Teachers can reset student LDAP passwords through the district admin panel. If you don’t have access to this tool, contact your school’s IT liaison. Students should never be given admin-level access to reset passwords themselves.

Is MyPascoConnect available over the summer?

Generally yes, but access to some tools may be limited during summer break. Canvas courses may be archived, and some apps may not be active until the new school year setup is complete. Contact IT for specifics.
