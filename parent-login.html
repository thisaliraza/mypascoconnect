MyPascoConnect Parent Login — myStudent & Focus Portal Guide

Parents and guardians use the myStudent Parent Portal (Focus School Software) — a separate system from the student MyPascoConnect ClassLink portal — to access student records and credentials.

Unofficial guide. Not affiliated with Pasco County Schools, ClassLink, or Focus School Software.

Important: Parents Use a Different Portal

Key distinction:
👤 Students → log in at MyPascoConnect ClassLink with their Student ID and LDAP password.
👪 Parents → log in at the myStudent parent portal powered by Focus School Software.
These are two completely separate systems. Parents cannot log into the student ClassLink portal.

What is the myStudent Parent Portal?

The myStudent parent portal is the official parent-facing platform for Pasco County Schools, powered by Focus School Software. It gives parents and guardians a secure window into their child’s academic life without needing to access the student’s own ClassLink account.

Through myStudent, parents can view real-time grades and GPA, attendance records, class schedules, teacher contact information, school calendar events, and lunch account balances. It also shows the student’s ID number, which can help if the child has forgotten their MyPascoConnect username.

How to Access myStudent (Step-by-Step)

1

Go to the myStudent Portal

Open your browser and navigate to the Focus School Software parent portal:

🌐 pasco.focusschoolsoftware.com
2

Create a Parent Account (New Users)

Click “Create a Parent Account” or “New User”. Enter your email address â€” use the same email you provided to the school during enrollment if possible.

3

Verify Your Email

Check your email inbox for a verification message from Pasco County Schools. Click the link inside to confirm your account. Check your spam folder if it doesn’t arrive within a few minutes.

4

Link Your Child’s Account

After verifying, you will be prompted to enter your child’s Student ID number and date of birth to link their school record to your parent account.

💡 If you don’t know your child’s Student ID, contact their school office. It is also printed on their student ID card.
5

Log In and Access Student Information

Once linked, you can log in with your email and password to see your child’s full academic profile at any time.

What Parents Can See in myStudent

📊

Grades & GPA

Real-time grades for all classes, GPA calculation, and historical report cards.

📅

Attendance

View your child’s daily attendance record including absences, tardies, and excused days.

📋

Class Schedule

Full semester schedule with teacher names, room numbers, and class times.

📧

Teacher Contact

Email your child’s teachers directly through the portal for progress updates.

👤

Student ID

View your child’s Student ID number — their MyPascoConnect username.

🆎

Lunch Balance

Check remaining lunch account balance and add funds if needed.

How to Find Your Child’s MyPascoConnect Login

If your child has forgotten their MyPascoConnect username, navigate to their profile in the myStudent portal. Their Student ID number (displayed there) is their ClassLink username.

For the LDAP password, parents cannot reset it directly — the child’s teacher must do this. Contact the school directly or have your child ask their teacher.

⚠️ Security tip: Never share your myStudent login credentials with your child or anyone else. Your parent account contains sensitive contact and academic information.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I log into my child’s MyPascoConnect account as a parent?

No. Parents cannot and should not log into the student ClassLink portal. You have your own separate parent portal (myStudent) that shows everything you need. Logging into a student account would be a security violation.

My email has changed. How do I update it?

Contact your child’s school office to update your email address in the district records. This will allow you to recover your myStudent account with the new email.

Can I add multiple children to one parent account?

Yes. After linking your first child, you can add additional children by going to your account settings and linking each child’s Student ID. All children will appear in your parent portal dashboard.

I can’t see my child’s information after linking. What now?

Make sure you entered the correct Student ID and date of birth. If the information is correct but records are not showing, contact your child’s school office — your account may need to be manually approved.

Is there a mobile app for the myStudent parent portal?

Focus School Software has a mobile-friendly website. Check the Google Play Store or Apple App Store for any official Focus Student or parent app. The web portal at pasco.focusschoolsoftware.com works well on mobile browsers.
