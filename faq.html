MyPascoConnect Frequently Asked Questions

20+ answers for Pasco County Schools students, parents, teachers, and staff — covering login, passwords, apps, Chromebook, mobile access, and more.

Unofficial guide. Not affiliated with Pasco County Schools or ClassLink. For official support, contact your school or IT help desk (813-794-2859).

General Questions

What is MyPascoConnect?

MyPascoConnect is the official Single Sign-On (SSO) portal for Pasco County Schools in Florida, built on the ClassLink platform. One login gives students, teachers, and staff access to Canvas MyLearning, Microsoft Office 365, Teams, digital textbooks, and more from a personalized LaunchPad.

Is MyPascoConnect the same as ClassLink?

Yes. MyPascoConnect is Pasco County Schools’ branded name for their ClassLink LaunchPad deployment. ClassLink is the technology company that provides the SSO platform. When you log in to MyPascoConnect, you are logging into ClassLink’s system.

Is MyPascoConnect free?

Yes. MyPascoConnect is provided free of charge to all Pasco County Schools students, teachers, and staff. There is no cost to sign in or use any of the apps on the LaunchPad.

What is Single Sign-On (SSO)?

SSO means you log in once with one username and password, and then access multiple apps — Canvas, Office 365, Teams — without signing in to each one separately. MyPascoConnect uses ClassLink to provide SSO, saving time and reducing password fatigue.

Login & Credentials

What is my MyPascoConnect username?

Students: Your username is your Student ID number (also called your lunch number) — usually 6–7 digits. Find it on your student ID card, report card, or ask your teacher.
Staff/Teachers: Your username is your district email address (e.g. firstname.lastname@pasco.k12.fl.us).

What is an LDAP password?

LDAP (Lightweight Directory Access Protocol) is the secure network system Pasco County Schools uses to manage all user accounts. Your LDAP password is assigned by your school and controls access to school computers, the network, and MyPascoConnect. Ask your teacher if you need a reset — students cannot self-reset this password.

Is my lunch number the same as my Student ID?

Yes. In Pasco County Schools, your lunch number and Student ID number are the same number — a unique 6–7 digit identifier used for school lunch, your student record, and as your MyPascoConnect username.

Can I log in from home?

Yes. MyPascoConnect is accessible 24 hours a day, 7 days a week from any internet-connected device. Open Google Chrome, go to login.classlink.com/my/dsbpc, and sign in with your credentials.

What browser should I use?

Google Chrome is the recommended browser. It provides the best compatibility with ClassLink and all LaunchPad apps. Safari, Firefox, and Edge may work but can cause issues with some apps.

Passwords

How do I reset my password?

Password reset depends on your role:
Students: Ask your classroom teacher — they can reset your LDAP password through the admin system.
Parents: Use the “Forgot Password” link on the myStudent portal.
Staff: Call the IT help desk at 813-794-2859.
See our password reset guide for detailed steps.

Why can’t I reset my own LDAP password?

The LDAP system is a centralized district directory controlled by Pasco County Schools IT. Student accounts are restricted for security. Only teachers and administrators can reset student passwords to protect the integrity of student data.

Apps & LaunchPad

What apps are on my LaunchPad?

Common apps include: Canvas MyLearning, Microsoft Office 365 (Word, Excel, PowerPoint), Microsoft Teams, OneDrive, Focus Gradebook, digital textbooks, and other district-approved tools. Your specific apps depend on your school, grade level, and role.

What is Canvas MyLearning?

Canvas (branded MyLearning in Pasco County Schools) is the district’s Learning Management System (LMS). Students submit assignments, check grades, view course materials, join discussions, and message teachers through Canvas.

Is Microsoft Office 365 free for students?

Yes. Microsoft Office 365 — including Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Teams, Outlook, and 1TB OneDrive storage — is free for all Pasco County Schools students and staff through the district’s Microsoft Education agreement.

Why is my LaunchPad empty?

An empty LaunchPad usually means apps have not yet been assigned to your account. This commonly happens at the start of a new school year. Contact your teacher or school office to have your apps provisioned. It can take 1–2 school days.

Parents & Guardians

Can parents log in to MyPascoConnect?

No. Parents use a separate system called myStudent (Focus School Software) at pasco.focusschoolsoftware.com. This shows grades, attendance, class schedule, and your child’s Student ID. It is completely separate from the student ClassLink portal.

What is the myStudent parent portal?

myStudent is the Pasco County Schools parent portal powered by Focus School Software. Parents can view their child’s grades, GPA, attendance record, class schedule, teacher contacts, and lunch account balance. See our parent login guide.

How do I find my child’s Student ID?

Your child’s Student ID appears on their student ID card, printed report cards, and in the myStudent parent portal under their profile. You can also request it from the school office.

Devices & Mobile

Does MyPascoConnect work on Chromebook?

Yes. Chromebooks with Google Chrome are the most popular student devices in Pasco County Schools. All LaunchPad apps work fully on Chrome OS. See our Chromebook guide.

Is there a MyPascoConnect mobile app?

There is no dedicated MyPascoConnect app. Instead, download the ClassLink app from the iOS App Store or Google Play. Log in with your Pasco County Schools credentials to access your LaunchPad on your phone or tablet. See our mobile app guide.

Does MyPascoConnect work on iPhone or iPad?

Yes. Download the ClassLink app from the Apple App Store. You can also use Safari or Chrome on iOS to access the portal, though the app provides a better experience.
