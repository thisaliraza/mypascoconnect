MyPascoConnect Apps — Your ClassLink LaunchPad Guide

Once you log in to MyPascoConnect, your personalized ClassLink LaunchPad gives you one-click access to every educational app your school has enabled — no extra passwords needed.

Unofficial guide. Not affiliated with Pasco County Schools, ClassLink, or any app publisher listed below.

How the ClassLink LaunchPad Works

After logging in to MyPascoConnect, your ClassLink LaunchPad loads — a personalized dashboard of app tiles configured by your school. Click any tile to open the app instantly using Single Sign-On (SSO) — no separate username or password required.

💡 Not all apps show for everyone. Your LaunchPad is personalized based on your role (student, teacher, staff), your school, and your grade level. Apps are assigned by school administrators at the start of each year.

Canvas MyLearning

Canvas (branded as MyLearning in Pasco County Schools) is the district’s Learning Management System (LMS). It is the central hub for all coursework.

What Students Can Do in Canvas

  • View all assignments, quizzes, and due dates on the Dashboard and To Do list
  • Submit assignments — upload files, type responses, or record video submissions
  • Check grades and feedback from teachers on each assignment
  • Join class Discussions and reply to posts
  • Watch lesson videos and view course materials posted by teachers
  • Send messages to teachers through the Canvas inbox
  • Join live class sessions using integrated video tools
📱 Download the Canvas Student app from the App Store or Google Play to get assignment notifications on your phone.

Microsoft Office 365

All Pasco County Schools students and staff have a free Microsoft 365 account accessible through their MyPascoConnect LaunchPad. This includes the full suite of productivity tools:

📃

Microsoft Word

Write essays, reports, and documents. Works fully in the browser or as a desktop app.

📈

Microsoft Excel

Spreadsheets, data tables, and math work. Available online and on desktop.

🎨

PowerPoint

Create slideshow presentations for class projects. Collaborate with classmates in real time.

📤

OneDrive

1 TB of cloud storage. Save files once, access them from any device at school or home.

💻

Microsoft Teams

Chat, video calls, and file sharing for class collaboration and virtual lessons.

📧

Outlook Email

School email account for communicating with teachers and receiving school notifications.

🌟 Students can also download Office desktop apps (Word, Excel, PowerPoint) for free on up to 5 personal devices using their school Microsoft account.

Microsoft Teams

Microsoft Teams is used by Pasco County Schools for virtual class meetings, teacher-to-student communication, and collaborative projects. Your school Microsoft 365 account gives you full access.

  • Join live class meetings via video call
  • Chat with teachers and classmates in class Teams channels
  • Submit assignments directly through Teams Assignments tab
  • Share and co-edit Office documents in Teams
  • Access Teams on Chromebook, iPhone, Android, or desktop

Focus Gradebook & myStudent

Focus School Software powers the district’s grade management and student information system.

For Students (Focus Gradebook)

  • View current grades for each class
  • Check your GPA and academic standing
  • Review your full class schedule
  • Track your attendance history

For Parents (myStudent)

Parents access a separate portal. See the parent login guide and the Focus parent portal guide for full details.

Digital Textbooks

Many Pasco County Schools classes use digital textbooks available directly through the ClassLink LaunchPad. These are publisher-provided books that load without any extra login. Access depends on your enrolled courses and teacher settings.

Why Some Apps Don’t Appear

An app I need isn’t on my LaunchPad

Apps are assigned by school administrators. If a tool you expect is missing, tell your teacher and they can request it be added to your profile, or you may need to be enrolled in the related class first.

An app shows an error when I click it

Log out of MyPascoConnect completely, close all browser tabs, and log back in. SSO tokens occasionally expire. If the error persists for a specific app, report it to your teacher.

My LaunchPad is empty after logging in

This often happens at the start of a new school year before apps are assigned, or if you are logging in with the wrong account type. Contact your teacher or school office.
